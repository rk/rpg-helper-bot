## Dice notation

Preserve dice notation exactly as written. Do not expand, normalize, or paraphrase dice expressions.

- `NdS` means roll N dice of S sides (e.g. `2D6` = two six-sided dice).
- `NdS+M` or `NdS-M` applies a flat modifier to the roll total (e.g. `2D6-2`).
- `d8` and `D8` are equivalent attribute/skill die sizes.
- Dice-pool systems may use `Nd` without a size when the die type is defined by context (e.g. `2D` = two dice of the system's standard die).
- In answers, repeat the user's dice notation when describing rolls.

## RPG terminology

Different games use different names for the same concepts (skill check, trait test, test, roll, etc.). Use glossary mappings when provided. Do not assume one game's jargon applies to another.
